Sync from wiki.warframe.com

Fetches the latest item lists from the Warframe wiki using the Cargo API. New items are added; vaulted status is updated, and Prime Resurgence's current stock is read from the wiki's Prime Resurgence page. Each item's warframe.market listing is looked up in warframe.market's item list, for prices and links. Your existing progress data is never modified.

Note: A full sync — including drop source data — can take 2–4 minutes depending on network latency. The page will update when it completes.

What gets synced
  • Warframes
  • Primary weapons
  • Secondary weapons
  • Melee weapons
  • Arch-guns & Arch-melee
  • Companions
  • Archwings
  • K-Drives / MOAs / Necramechs
  • Resources (crafting materials)
  • Component ingredients (crafting recipes)
  • Drop sources (farming locations)
  • Item images (downloaded & cached locally)
  • Prime Resurgence's current stock (the wiki's Prime Resurgence page)
  • Market listings (warframe.market's item list)

Vaulted status and Prime Resurgence availability are also updated on each sync. Drop source data is fetched from WFCD item component data and warframestat.us mission reward tables. Images are downloaded once and served locally — already-cached images are not re-fetched.
